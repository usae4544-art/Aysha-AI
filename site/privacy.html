Privacy Architecture

AYSHA Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Local-First Storage & Hardware Encryption

AYSHA stores your chats, memories, personal documents, custom rules, and workflows locally on your Android device inside a private Room SQLite database. Your BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) API keys for Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI-compatible providers, or local Ollama are encrypted using AndroidKeyStore AES-256-GCM and are never sent to any AYSHA developer server.

2. Microphone, Wake Word & Awareness Gate

Optional on-device wake-word detection runs locally via the bundled Vosk acoustic recognizer. Audio is only sent to your chosen cloud speech or AI provider when you explicitly trigger voice input or wake AYSHA. When Mute During Media is enabled, wake detection pauses automatically while music or video plays.

3. Explicit Consent for Sensitive Actions

  • Calls, SMS & Emergency SOS: Always prompt an explicit confirmation dialog before dialing or sending SMS.
  • Accessibility & Notifications: Remain completely disabled until you explicitly toggle them in Android System Settings.
  • Camera & Photos: Used solely when you capture or select an image in the Visual Scanner or Chat composer.

4. Full Control & Data Wipe

You can export chats, restore safe JSON backups (API keys are never included in exports), delete stored API keys, or wipe all local data at any time from Privacy, Backup & SOS inside the app.
