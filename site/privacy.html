Privacy policy

How AYSHA AI handles authentication, licensing records, and local device intelligence.

1. Local-first voice & memory storage

All conversational chat history, rules, documents, and Room database memories live on your device. Provider API keys and your Firebase refresh token are encrypted with AndroidKeyStore AES-256-GCM before storage.

2. Google Sign-In & license verification

When you sign in with Google via browser OAuth, AYSHA stores only your Firebase UID, verified email address, display name, license tier, coupon redemption record, and any submitted UPI UTR payment references in Firestore strictly to enforce active app licensing.

3. Direct AI provider traffic

Requests to Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, or local Ollama endpoints go directly from your Android device using the keys you configure. Safe JSON backups exclude all API keys and authentication tokens.
