Core Architecture

Built end-to-end for real phone control and natural Hinglish voice

Every layer runs cleanly in native Kotlin and Jetpack Compose with offline-first fallbacks and explicit user control.

Voice & Awareness Engine

Speaker Barge-In, Follow-Up Window & On-Device Wake Word

Talk naturally without repeating "Hey Aysha" on every turn. AYSHA monitors speaker barge-in using Android hardware AcousticEchoCanceler & NoiseSuppressor, respects media/TV mute preferences, and processes wake phrases locally via Vosk with Reserved, Balanced, and Alert sensitivity profiles.

Gemini Live Audio Vosk KWS AEC3 / NS Barge-In Decision Log
Semantic Memory & Rules

Hinglish TF-IDF Recall + AYSHA Rules

Stores memories in Room SQLite with source tags, archive/restore, document chunking, and user-defined behavior rules (e.g. "Ab se hamesha chhote jawab dena") prioritized directly in the system prompt.

Theme & Surface Engine

Obsidian, Midnight, Clay & Inter/Mono

Switch seamlessly across 9 palettes, 4 card surfaces (Glass, Clay, Flat, Soft), 4 corner radii, UI scaling, and bundled SIL OFL Inter, Nunito, and JetBrains Mono typefaces.

Vision, Tutor & Browser

Scanner, Study Whiteboard & Mini Browser

Capture or pick photos to solve questions visually, learn step-by-step on the dark Study Whiteboard with code blocks, or summarize any webpage from the built-in browser.

Phone & Accessibility

Smart Macros & Confirmation Safety

Open apps, toggle flashlight, set reminders, inspect screens, and run multi-step macros. Sensitive actions like Calls, SMS, and Emergency SOS always require explicit confirmation.

Privacy & BYOK

Your keys, your data, encrypted on your phone

Bring your own free Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI-compatible, or local Ollama endpoint—or run 100% offline with zero keys. All secrets are encrypted inside hardware-backed AndroidKeyStore AES-256-GCM and redacted from logs and JSON backups.
